Product Series

产品系列

FUKA

High Precision Micro Diameter Endmills

高精度微径铣刀系列

FUKA cutting tools are designed for precision mold, automotive, aerospace and medical part machining. The series covers hardened steel and copper-aluminum applications, with micro diameter and long neck options for fine details and deep features.

FUKA 刀具系列面向精密模具、汽车、航空及医疗零件加工，覆盖淬硬钢及铜铝合金应用，并提供微径及长颈规格，适合细节位、深沟及高精度加工。

  • FX6M hardened steel series for up to 65HRC machining.
  • FX6M 淬硬钢系列，最高适用於 65HRC 加工。
  • FX3 copper and aluminum alloy series with high welding resistance.
  • FX3 铜铝合金系列，具备高抗焊接性及顺畅排屑。
  • Micro diameter lineup from Ø0.03mm for precision details.
  • 微径产品最小 Ø0.03mm，适合精密细节加工。
